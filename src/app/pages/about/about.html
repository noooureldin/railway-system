About EuroRail

Making European train travel simple, transparent and enjoyable.

EuroRail was built for travellers who love the journey as much as the destination. We bring together high-speed, regional and night train operators from across the continent into a single, easy search — so you can plan a trip from Lisbon to Ljubljana without juggling a dozen different national booking sites.
